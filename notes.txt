The solution (MocoTrajectory) does not contain actuation force/moment; they are control (e.g. [-1,+1])

To reduce the residual forces/moments, must either increase the weight of the corresponding actuators (with high optimal force), or use very weak residual actuators (e.g. 1Nm with 1 controls)

Slack variables (gamma) only appear if there are kinematic constraints in the model.

The weight of the controls must be low, otherwise, it changes the motion significantly.
