The solution (MocoTrajectory) does not contain actuation force/moment; they are control, e.g. [-1,+1].

There are two options for reducing residual forces/moments: either use very weak residual actuators, e.g. with 1N(m) optimal force with 1 controls, or use strong residual actuators, then increase their weight in the control-effort goal. The later one is more efficient computationally (less iterations, objective, and NLP error).

Slack variables (gamma) only appear if there are kinematic constraints in the model.

The weight of the controls must be low, otherwise, it changes the motion significantly.
